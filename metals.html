How these numbers work
Metal prices are COMEX futures from Yahoo Finance via a free relay, converted at live USD rates — they are the international benchmark, not a dealer's counter price. A jeweller or bullion dealer will always quote higher. Tax and duty figures below are editable assumptions, not live data; verify against CBIC and IRAS before any large purchase.
